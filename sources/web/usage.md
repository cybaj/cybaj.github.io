# web — Usage

## Item-wide research

Collect the authoritative primary documents for the item's topic — the official
docs, the specification, the canonical announcement. These are shared across
every post the item yields, so harvest them once at the `gathering` stage.

## Per-post research

Fetch the specific article a single post argues with or against. Keep it, even
if the post ends up disagreeing — the reference is what makes the disagreement
checkable.

## Cautions

- **Drift.** Pages change. `retrieved` is not decoration; re-check anything
  load-bearing before publishing.
- **Copyright.** Reference files are working material, not publishable content.
  Quote briefly and cite; do not paste a whole article into a post.
- **Authority.** A blog post and a specification are not equal evidence. Prefer
  primary sources, and say when you are relying on someone's opinion.
